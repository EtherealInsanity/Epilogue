Instant Loading for Massive Volumes

Zero Lag

Never wait on loading screens. Even massive 1,000+ chapter web novels and 100MB+ EPUBs open in milliseconds. Epilogue seamlessly prepares upcoming chapters so your reading flow is never broken.

📖

Scroll or Turn Pages

Read whichever way feels natural. Choose smooth continuous vertical scrolling, or tap to flip pages with optional hardware volume key turning.

🎨

Original Book Typography

Faithfully preserves publisher formatting, drop caps, poetry alignment, custom scene dividers, and artistic quotes just as the book designer intended.

🖼️

Inspect Illustrations & Maps

Double-tap or pinch to smoothly examine fantasy world maps, intricate character art, and high-resolution book illustrations with fluid pan and zoom.

🖍️

Highlights & Notes

Highlight memorable lines in five soothing pastel colors, attach personal thoughts, and instantly search through your reflections anytime.

🛡️

100% Private, Offline & Zero Ads

No accounts, no logins, no analytics tracking, and absolutely no banner ads or paywalls. Epilogue works entirely on your local device and never uploads your library or reading history anywhere.
