[ DISTRACTION-FREE READING ]

Engineered for Serious Readers

Built specifically for long binge-reading sessions. Everything you need to get lost in a good story, with zero clutter.

Instant Loading for Huge Books

Zero Lag

Never wait on loading screens. Even massive 1,000+ chapter web novels and 100MB+ EPUBs open in milliseconds. Epilogue smoothly prepares upcoming chapters so your reading flow is never interrupted.

📖

Scroll or Turn Pages

Read whichever way feels natural. Choose smooth continuous vertical scrolling, or tap to flip pages with optional volume key turning.

🎨

Preserves Book Typography

Preserves custom author typography, scene break dividers, centered dialogue, and artistic quotes just as the publisher intended.

🖼️

Inspect Illustrations & Maps

Double-tap or pinch to smoothly examine fantasy maps, character portraits, and high-resolution book illustrations.

🖍️

Highlights & Notes

Highlight memorable lines in five soothing pastel colors, attach personal thoughts, and search through your notes anytime.

🛡️

100% Private & Ad-Free

No accounts, no analytics tracking, no banner ads, and no pop-ups. Epilogue runs completely on your own device and never uploads your reading history anywhere.

Serene Library

Tailored Typography

Distraction-Free Immersion

📑

Effortless Navigation

📦

Always Offline, Always Yours

Import your EPUB books directly and read anywhere—on trains, flights, or in remote spots without an internet connection.
