[ CORE PHILOSOPHY ]

The Reading Experience is Sacred

Reading an epic story should never feel like browsing a commercial web app. Epilogue strips away the noise to leave only the words and the story.

📖

Zero Ads & Zero Telemetry

100% offline-first. No advertisements, no paywalls, no behavioral tracking, and no external telemetry leaving your device. Your reading habits belong strictly to you.

📜

Pure Paper Typography

Crafted around a zero-yellowing paper aesthetic (#FFFFFF surface with #111827 deep ink). Crisp serif and sans-serif typography, customizable line spacing, and margin padding.

🌑

True OLED Pitch-Black

A deep, absolute black theme (#000000) engineered specifically for night-time reading sessions on modern AMOLED screens, eliminating battery drain and blue-light strain.

[ ARCHITECTURE & ENGINE ]

Built for Massive 500+ Chapter Epics

Engineered from scratch in Flutter to eliminate common mobile reader flaws and handle multi-volume giants without dropping a single frame.

📐

Dynamic Viewport Character Budgeting

Most mobile readers leave up to 40% of the screen blank on modern 20:9 and 21:9 displays due to hardcoded character counts. Epilogue calculates live character and line capacities directly from your display geometry:

$$\text{Budget} = \lfloor \text{Usable Height} / \text{Line Height} \rfloor \times \lfloor \text{Usable Width} / (\text{Font Size} \times 0.52) \rfloor$$

Text breaks naturally at paragraph and sentence endings, completely filling the screen from top to bottom.

🎨

EPUB CSS Engine

Extracts embedded stylesheets and <style> blocks on-the-fly. Preserves headers, centered text, blockquotes, and dividers without tearing HTML tags across page flips.

📺

Retro CRT Scanline Shader

Powered by a custom GLSL fragment shader (shaders/crt.frag). Adds an optional vintage CRT scanline overlay with subtle phosphor glow, bringing a cyberpunk terminal ambiance to your reading.

🖼️

Inline Image Pinch-to-Zoom

Inspect maps, character illustrations, and volume covers in rich detail with interactive multi-touch pinch-to-zoom (0.8x to 4.0x) powered by Flutter's InteractiveViewer.

🖍️

5 Star Stream Highlight Palettes

Categorize quotes, inner monologues, and epic moments using canon color palettes with attached notes and filterable archives:
