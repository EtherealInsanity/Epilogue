Built for Readers, Not Engagement Metrics

Most reading apps try to squeeze you for micro-transactions or force 30-second video ads between chapters. Epilogue is completely free, offline-first, and respects your peace of mind.

🚫

Zero Ads, Zero Subscriptions

No coin wallets. No unlock countdowns. No full-screen popups right when the protagonist is about to unleash their ultimate move. Just your books and pure peace.

📖

Paper-Clean Typography

Zero weird yellow tints. High-contrast ink text (#111827) on crisp paper white (#FFFFFF), with customizable line heights, fonts, and edge margins.

🌑

OLED Black for 3 AM Binges

Telling yourself "just one more chapter" at 2:00 AM? The true pitch-black AMOLED theme shuts off your screen pixels completely to save battery and spare your retinas.

[ UNDER THE HOOD ]

Engineered to Handle Massive 90MB+ EPUBs

Opening a 551-chapter epic with hundreds of embedded illustrations usually crashes standard readers. Epilogue was tuned specifically so it never drops a frame.

📐

No More Awkward Empty Bottom Gaps

Ever notice how reader apps on tall 20:9 or 21:9 phones leave the bottom 35% of the screen totally blank? That happens because of hardcoded character limits. Epilogue dynamically calculates line and character capacities live from your actual screen geometry:

Budget = floor(Height / LineHeight) × floor(Width / (FontSize × 0.52))

Text breaks at natural sentences and fills your entire screen cleanly from top to bottom.

🎨

EPUB Styles That Don't Tear

Renders original chapter headers, blockquotes, centered dialogue, and dividers faithfully without slicing HTML tags across page flips.

📺

Retro CRT Scanline Shader

Toggle the custom GLSL fragment shader (shaders/crt.frag) whenever you want your novel to look like an old-school cyberpunk terminal.

🖼️

Pinch-to-Zoom Illustrations

Pinch, pan, and zoom character illustrations and world maps from 0.8x up to 4.0x with smooth multi-touch gestures.

🖍️

5 Highlight Color Palettes
