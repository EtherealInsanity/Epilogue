[ DISTRACTION-FREE READING ]

Engineered for Serious Readers

Built specifically for long binge-reading sessions. Everything you need to get lost in a good story, with zero clutter.

Instant Loading for Huge Books

Zero Lag

Never wait on loading screens. Even massive 1,000+ chapter web novels and 100MB+ EPUBs open in the blink of an eye. Epilogue loads upcoming chapters quietly in the background as you read, keeping memory usage light and battery life high.

📖

Scroll or Turn Pages

Read whichever way feels best. Choose smooth continuous vertical scrolling, or tap to flip pages with optional volume key turning.

🎨

Keeps Original Book Styles

Preserves custom author typography, scene break dividers, centered dialogue, and quotes just as the publisher intended.

🖼️

Pinch to Zoom Pictures

Double-tap or pinch to smoothly inspect maps, character profiles, and high-resolution book illustrations.

🖍️

Highlights & Notes

Highlight memorable lines in five soothing colors, attach personal thoughts, and search through your notes anytime.

🛡️

100% Private & Ad-Free

No account creation, no analytics tracking, no banner ads, and no pop-ups. Epilogue runs completely on your own device and never uploads your reading history anywhere.

[ BUILT-IN BOOK STUDIO ]

Create & Edit Books Easily

Write your own stories or tweak books in your existing library with simple, powerful editing tools.

Simple, Friendly Formatting

Visual Editor ⇄ Markdown Mode

Write and format your chapters without getting bogged down in code. Use familiar buttons for bold, italics, headings, and quotes, or switch over to clean markdown whenever you want to type fast.

✏️

Edit Books in Your Library

Fix annoying typos, rename chapter headings, or add missing book covers to any book already saved in your app.

📑

Drag & Drop Chapters

Rearrange chapter order effortlessly with a finger or mouse, see chapter word counts at a glance, and duplicate sections in one tap.

🛡️

Automatic Error Checking

The built-in checker automatically detects unclosed formatting tags or empty chapters before you save, keeping your books error-free.

📦

Works on Any E-Reader

Exports clean, standard EPUB books that open properly on Kindle, Kobo, Apple Books, and Android readers without formatting glitches.
